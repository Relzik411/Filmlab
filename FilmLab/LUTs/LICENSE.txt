The .cube files in this folder are adapted from the RawTherapee Film Simulation Collection
(version 2015-09-20) by Pat David, Pavlov Dmitry and Michael Ezra:
https://rawpedia.rawtherapee.com/Film_Simulation

Changes: converted from level-12 Hald CLUT PNGs to 33x33x33 .cube files with trilinear
resampling (scripts/hald_to_cube.py) and renamed. Each file's header names its source image.
