FilmLab's looks come from three sources. Each .cube file's header names its source.

1. RawTherapee Film Simulation Collection (version 2015-09-20)
   by Pat David, Pavlov Dmitry and Michael Ezra
   https://rawpedia.rawtherapee.com/Film_Simulation
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

2. PictureFX CLUTs by Marc Roovers (www.digicrea.be)
   (Heat, Breeze, Sepia, Antique, Silver)
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

   Changes to 1 and 2: converted from level-12 Hald CLUT PNGs to 33x33x33 .cube files with trilinear
   resampling (scripts/hald_to_cube.py) and renamed. These adapted files are shared under the same
   licence. Film stock names appear only in the source notes, to say which stock a look approximates;
   no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

3. FilmLab originals (1975, Memory, Sunbleached, Expired, Tide, Disposable)
   Generated by scripts/make_vintage_luts.py.
   Licence: CC0 1.0, dedicated to the public domain
   https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

looks.json sets how the looks are grouped and ordered in the app.
